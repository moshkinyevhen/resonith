## Alternatives considered

### Direct Truth

Always retained. It is the only currently admitted result on the frozen long
input.

### Frame-local multiple fundamentals

Rejected as the primary architecture. It can choose subharmonics, duplicate
leaked peaks, switch source identity, and exclude inharmonic paths before byte
RDO.

### First-order global peak matching

Accepted only as a narrow clean-partial diagnostic. Rejected as a committing
source or codec selector because local peak visibility, first-order edge cost,
and frame-level phase do not solve crossings, weak-under-strong signals,
transients, noise, or actual byte selection.

### Complex factorization or neural separation

Retained as optional proposers. They can expose mixture structure but are
non-identifiable, may over-separate, and optimize a proxy unless every emitted
state and final Truth is charged.

### Time-domain convolutional sparse coding and direct long Basis

Retained as independent proposers. They preserve phase naturally and can model
events hidden from spectral peaks, but dictionary learning and sparse selection
are expensive and still require complete decoder-domain RDO.

### Revised union

The proposed Foundry union contains multiresolution complex-partial paths,
time-domain sparse Basis, complex factorization, optional learned proposers,
transient/stochastic/resonator candidates, and direct Truth. No proposer may
remove another declared family.

## Independent audit findings

The red-team reproduced four decisive counterexamples against the draft:

1. Two crossing mono chirps, 300 to 1100 Hz and 1100 to 300 Hz, produced three
   tracks rather than two. Mean track phase error was 0.9168 radians and one
   source split into 86- and 83-observation paths.
2. A 440 Hz tone at amplitude 12,000 plus a 2,000 Hz tone at amplitude 50
   produced only 4 weak-tone observations from 188 possible; amplitude 100
   produced 6. A frame-relative threshold therefore destroys weak-under-strong
   recall.
3. Half a second of white noise produced 9,072 observations and 95,660 edges,
   required the greedy fallback, and took 3.49 seconds. A one-second combined
   stress run exceeded 40 seconds.
4. A clean 440.3 Hz positive control produced one 189-observation track with
   0.0257-radian phase error. The narrow clean-single-partial premise is valid;
   the general architecture claim is not.

The code audit also found:

- sub-bin frequency was paired with integer-bin complex phase;
- synthesis restarted phase per hop without terminal-phase constraints;
- arbitrary matching weights omitted node, birth, death, minimum-length,
  record, and final-Truth costs;
- post-solve deletion of short paths could block useful alternatives;
- energy ranking replaced codec RDO;
- the draft had one resolution, no uncertainty, no residual re-observation,
  no route law, no cause grouping, no final Truth pack, and no complete-byte
  gate.

## Required revised design

1. Estimate complex amplitude and phase at the fitted frequency with a direct
   complex DTFT or validated reassignment, including variance, SNR, resolution,
   and resolvability metadata.
2. Form the union of overlapping resolutions, per-band noise-adaptive
   observations, per-channel/spatial observations, and decoder-residual
   observations.
3. Preserve top-K second-order continuation hypotheses through crossings and
   gaps. Exact first-order flow remains an audit subproblem, not a general
   optimum claim.
4. Charge continuation against restart, birth, death, minimum lifetime,
   parameter records, and estimated Truth consequence. Actual selection still
   requires pack, native decode, one final Truth, and complete bytes.
5. Compile phase only through an independently audited endpoint-constrained
   phase/frequency law with sparse innovations. Until that law exists, the
   graph is analyzer-only.
6. Keep independent channel states as the honest fallback. Delay, polarity,
   decay, and frequency-dependent transfer sharing require their own charged
   transport and audit.
7. Group paths only after tracking and only when a harmonic, inharmonic,
   common-modulation, envelope, resonator, motif, or route law is cheaper than
   the independent paths.
8. Move the sparse graph and fitting kernels to C++23/CUDA before any
   long-real gate. Python remains the oracle and report control plane.

## Mandatory counterexample gate

- exact sub-bin sinusoid;
- two resolvable and two unresolvable close tones;
- weak partial below a strong partial;
- identifiable crossing chirps;
- short and long gaps with continuation-versus-restart accounting;
- opposite polarity;
- known integer and fractional cross-channel delay;
- transient overlap;
- deterministic plus stochastic overlap;
- white and colored noise scaling;
- exact-versus-bounded solver gap on tractable subgraphs.

Every row reports observation recall under an explicit resolvability mask,
track-equivalence identity switches, endpoint phase error, route error,
predictor/program/Truth/complete bytes, decoded hash, runtime, peak memory, and
fallback status.
