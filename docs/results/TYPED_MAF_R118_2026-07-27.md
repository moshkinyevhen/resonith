## Scope

This gate evaluates the first complete native-decoded `MFT1` predictor plus
lapped Truth candidate against the exact preceding Resonith stream on the
non-negotiable R-118 union:

- complete LibriSpeech reference;
- complete Emotional piano reference;
- complete 400.773-second Mozart overture;
- all 16 prepared R-111 heterogeneous classes.

Every candidate includes the complete outer `RSC1`, `CONF`, `MFT1`, and nested
Truth bytes. Every evaluated PCM array comes from the independent C++23
decoder. A candidate is selected only when it is no larger than the preceding
complete stream and passes the R-135 waveform and spectral non-regression
guard. Speech items also retain the established speech-metric path.

This run does not compare a newly admitted default against Opus because no new
candidate was admitted. Existing selected files remain the previously
published Resonith fallbacks.

## What the gate proves

The electronic-tune point is the first large complete-byte MAF opportunity:
three periodic, 13 impulse, and 21 stochastic lifetimes plus a 12-coefficient
Truth residual reduced rate by 68.38%. It was correctly rejected because its
spectral quality failed every multiresolution STFT guard as well as log-mel
and magnitude similarity. This is not a codec win.

Five items, including complete Mozart and pinned speech, passed the declared
quality guard but lost on complete bytes. Complete Mozart used 1,720 impulse
lifetimes and 1,620 `NO_MODEL` router segments; its 69,032 predictor bytes and
larger residual outweighed the small quality improvement. Pinned speech chose
no MAF lifetime at all and lost only the experimental envelope overhead.

The current bottleneck is therefore twofold:

1. residual allocation optimizes waveform SSE rather than the admission
   spectral objective;
2. fixed 240 ms full-band routing repeats short records and cannot merge
   persistent states or make band-local representation decisions.

The next gate first searches the existing residual frontier under the R-135
quality constraints. Only after that measured search may persistence merging
or band-local syntax be justified.
